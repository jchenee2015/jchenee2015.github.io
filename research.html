ELISA: ELectrified and Intelligent Systems Automation Lab

The ELectrified and Intelligent Systems Automation Lab (ELISA) at Oakland University was established in 2020. ELISA Lab develops learning-enabled estimation and control for electrified and intelligent systems. Our guiding question is how to do more with less: fewer sensors, less communication, less computation, and less data, without giving up performance or safety guarantees. We combine control theory, optimization, formal methods, and machine learning to build scalable, verifiable algorithms for large-scale interconnected cyber-physical systems, from battery packs and power electronics to fleets of autonomous vehicles and robots.

       

Research Areas

Physical AI & Verifiable Autonomy: Learning where it helps, guarantees where they matter.

  • Learning-enabled estimation and control

  • Formal methods and discrete event systems

  • Safe decision making for autonomous systems

  • Multi-robot coordination

Estimation & Control of Interconnected Systems: Scalable theory for systems with many coupled parts.

  • Distributed and decentralized state estimation

  • Sensor-lean estimation

  • Model predictive and event-triggered control

  • Large-scale numerical optimization

Electrified & Intelligent Systems: Where the methods meet hardware.

  • EV battery management and second-life batteries

  • Power converter control and hybrid energy systems

  • Autonomous vehicles and ground mobile robots

  • Personalized mobility
